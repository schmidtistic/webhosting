    • Biogorphy, Chicago, IL

      Written on May 1, 2026.

      I was born in Chicago on June 30, 1956, the second of four children; Maryellen was born on May 20, 1954, then me, then Karen on June 13, 1958, and James (Jamie) on June 1, 1960. Maryellen, Karen, and I were all born in Chicago. Jamie was born in Pittsburgh.

      When we first moved to Pittsburgh, we lived at 933 Killarney Drive. It was a duplex, a few hundred feet from the trolley line, with a railroad track running just above it. One of my earliest memories was walking with my mother, Joan, down to the overpass to pick up Maryellen from kindergarten. I'm guessing that I was three years old, going on four.

      When I was four years old, we moved to a rented house off of Rt. 885, on Hollywood Drive. I had two friends that I remember, Tim Tankosic and Gary Davodic. There was also a bully named Howard Burkhart. He was a good-for-nothing. Years later, I was told that he died in Vietnam, but I searched the names on the Vietnam Memorial online, which identifies those killed in action, or missing, and his name didn't appear. I will talk more about Howard Burkhart later. I'm not sure of the spelling of his name, but the Memorial website lists names with various spellings. This is a terrible thing to say, but for years, I was glad to hear he was killed in Vietnam. Then, I was disappointed when his name didn't appear on any list.
