Why It Feels This Way

This is not a memorial. It is a living archive.

The older pieces deserve care. The newer pieces deserve honesty. The recordings deserve to feel present. The site should hold all three without flattening them into one mood.

SchmidtWorx exists to document, preserve, and pass forward what matters without sanding off the human edges.
